Score

Play it slow. Loop it. Own it.

A Guitar Pro player built for practice.

Free. No account. No AI. Works offline.

Swipe up

The thing every teacher repeats

100%

is the wrong speed to learn a song at.

Fingers learn what they repeat. Play it fast and messy and that is what they keep. Play it slow and clean, then raise the tempo a little at a time.

Tempo

Any speed from a quarter to double

The pitch never changes. The real BPM is always shown next to the percentage.

50%Learn the notes
75%Clean it up
100%Song tempo
110%Now it feels easy

Steps of 5%, from 25% to 200%. Press - and + on a keyboard, 0 to go back to 100%.

Loop

Repeat only the hard bars

With the mouse or a finger
Drag across the bars in the music.
With numbers
Type "from bar 9 to bar 12" in the Loop panel.
While it plays
Press [ where it starts and ] where it ends.

Time

A metronome that follows the song

Turn the click on and it follows every tempo and time signature change in the file, at whatever speed you chose.

Count-in gives you one bar of clicks before the music starts, so your hands are ready when the first note lands.

M for metronome. C for count-in.

Tracks

You are the mixing desk

Mute
Silence the guitar and play its part yourself over the band.
Solo
Hear one instrument alone to work out what it really plays.
Volume
A slider for every track, from silent up to 150%.
Show
Tick the tracks you want to read. One, several, or all.

Library

It remembers where you were

Every file you open is kept on your device. Open it again from the Library with no internet at all.

Score also remembers how you practiced each song: the tempo, the loop, which tracks were muted, soloed and shown.

Come back tomorrow and carry on at 70%, bars 9 to 12.

Nothing is sent anywhere

There is no server

No account, no sign up, no tracking, no AI. Your tabs are read by your own browser and never uploaded.

After the first visit Score works with the plane mode on. Add it to your home screen or Dock and it opens like any other app.

Your files are nobody else's data.

One thing to know

The library lives in your browser. Clear your browser data and the saved songs are gone.

Keep your original Guitar Pro files somewhere safe.

Score is a place to practice them, not the only copy.
