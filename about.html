Score

Everything Score does.

A free practice player for Guitar Pro and MuseScore files.

Free. No account. No AI. Works offline. Runs in any modern browser on a phone, tablet or computer.

Swipe up

What it is

A player made for learning songs

Score opens the tab files you already have and plays them back, with the tab and the notation scrolling along as it plays.

Everything that matters when you practise is one tap away: tempo, loop, metronome, and a mute and solo button for every instrument.

Loop

Repeat only the hard bars

With the mouse or a finger
Drag across the bars in the music.
With numbers
Type "from bar 9 to bar 12" in the Loop panel, or use This bar.
While it plays
Press [ where it starts and ] where it ends.

L turns the loop on and off, Esc clears it. Combine it with a slower tempo and work up from there.

Time

Metronome and count-in

The metronome clicks along and follows every tempo and time signature change in the file, at whatever speed you chose.

Count-in gives one bar of clicks before the music starts, so your hands are ready when the first note lands.

M for metronome. C for count-in.

MuseScore

Straight from the MuseScore folder

Open the .mscz that MuseScore saves, usually in Documents, MuseScore4, Scores. No export step.

Notes and rhythms, tab with strings and frets, bends, slides, hammer-ons, let ring, palm mute, lyrics, chord symbols, dynamics, tempo, repeats and endings all come across.

Score lays out the music itself, so MuseScore's page breaks and fonts are not copied. Zoom and Bars per row work the same as for Guitar Pro files.

Library

It remembers where you were

Every file you open is kept on your device. Open it again from the Library with no internet at all. Songs that are open in a tab are marked.

For each song Score remembers the tempo, the loop, the bars per row, and which tracks were muted, soloed, turned down and shown.

Come back tomorrow and carry on at 70%, bars 9 to 12.

Anywhere

Offline, and installable

Offline
After the first visit, Score works in plane mode. Songs, sounds and settings are all on the device.
Phones and tablets
iPhone and iPad: Safari, Share, Add to Home Screen. Android: Chrome menu, Install app.
Mac and PC
In Chrome or Edge, use the install button in the address bar. In Safari on a Mac, File, Add to Dock.

Updates arrive by themselves. Score tells you when to reload.

Privacy

Good to know

A few honest details

The library lives in this browser. Clearing browser data removes it, so keep your original files somewhere safe.
Each browser and device has its own library. Open the file there once and it is kept.
The first visit downloads the sounds once, about 17.5 MB, so do it on Wi-Fi.
